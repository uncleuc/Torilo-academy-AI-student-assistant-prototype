## Enrollment Requirements

To be admitted into an Academy programme, the Student or Enrolling Party must satisfy applicable enrollment requirements.

These may include:

- Completion of enrollment documentation
- Accurate and verifiable personal information
- Applicable academic information
- Applicable contact information
- Meeting minimum age requirements
- Meeting programme eligibility requirements
- Meeting applicable prerequisites
- Payment of applicable fees or deposits
- Providing requested supporting documentation
- Compliance with applicable legal or immigration requirements where relevant

## Admission Is Not Guaranteed

Admission into the Academy is not automatic or guaranteed.

The Academy may consider factors including:

- Programme capacity
- Class-size limits
- Academic history
- Behavioural history
- Programme eligibility
- Other institutional considerations

The Academy reserves the right to deny, defer, or revoke admission where applicable requirements are not satisfied or where false or misleading information has been provided.

## Course Registration

Enrollment in the Academy does not automatically entitle a student to participate in every course.

Students must follow the registration procedures applicable to each course or programme.

Registration is subject to factors including:

- Course availability
- Capacity
- Prerequisites
- Payment status
- Applicable registration procedures

## Prerequisites

Some courses may require:

- Completion of prerequisite modules
- Baseline assessments
- Proof of technical competence
- Other applicable qualifications

If a student does not meet the applicable requirements, the Academy may deny registration or recommend an alternate placement.

## Enrollment and Payment

Enrollment is not finalized until applicable registration fees, course fees, or initial deposits have been paid according to the Academy's payment terms or agreed payment plan.

Proof of payment may be required.

## Supporting Documents

The Academy may request documents including:

- Government-issued identification
- Academic certificates
- Passport photographs
- Signed enrollment documentation

Failure to submit required documentation may delay or prevent access to courses or Academy resources.

## Important Information Rule

Specific admission or prerequisite requirements must be taken from the relevant approved course or programme information.

The chatbot should not assume that a requirement for one programme applies to every programme.

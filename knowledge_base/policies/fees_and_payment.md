## Course Fees

Applicable fees for a course or programme are outlined in the specific Course Registration Form or invoice.

Students and Enrolling Parties are responsible for making payments according to the Academy's applicable payment policy and agreed payment terms.

## Payment Requirements

Enrollment may require payment of applicable:

- Registration fees
- Course fees
- Initial deposits
- Other applicable programme charges

Proof of payment may be required.

## Late Payment

Late payments may result in:

- Penalties
- Suspension from classes
- Withholding of results
- Withholding of certificates
- Withholding of letters of completion

These restrictions may remain in effect until the applicable outstanding payment is resolved.

## Financial Clearance

Students must obtain financial clearance where required in order to:

- Participate in final assessments
- Receive certificates
- Re-enrol in subsequent programmes

## Sponsored Students

Where a student is sponsored by an employer, donor, or another third party, the Student or Enrolling Party remains responsible for ensuring that required payments are made on time.

Failure by a sponsor to fulfill financial obligations may result in responsibility reverting to the Student and/or Enrolling Party.

## Changes to Fees

The Academy reserves the right to review and revise its fee structure periodically.

Any increase in tuition or other fees will be communicated in writing before the start of a new enrollment cycle or academic term.

The agreement states that fee changes do not apply retroactively to programmes already in progress unless explicitly stated.

## Refunds

Fees are generally non-refundable except as provided under the Academy's applicable Refund Policy.

Specific refund conditions are documented separately in:

`policies/refund_policy.md`

## Current Prices

The chatbot should not treat this document as a source for current promotional prices, discounts, or cohort-specific tuition unless those amounts are explicitly included in an approved current source.

Current fees should be retrieved from the appropriate current fee information when available.

## Important Information Rule

The chatbot must not invent or estimate course fees.

If the knowledge base does not contain a current fee for a specific programme, the chatbot should state that the current fee is not available in the approved knowledge base and direct the student to the appropriate Academy contact.

# Torilo Academy — Certification Policy

- Document ID: policy_certification

- Organization: Torilo Academy
- Source: Master Enrollment Agreement
- Last Verified: October 2026
- Status: Active

## Overview

Successful completion of a Torilo Academy course or programme depends on meeting the applicable academic and administrative requirements.

Enrollment alone does not automatically entitle a student to a certificate.

## Conditions for Completion and Certification

The Master Enrollment Agreement identifies the following conditions for certification:

- Successful completion of course requirements
- Completion of assignments
- Completion of projects
- Completion of assessments
- Meeting applicable attendance thresholds
- Compliance with academic integrity requirements
- Compliance with conduct standards
- Full settlement of applicable financial obligations
- Completion of any applicable capstone project
- Completion of any applicable final evaluation or practicum

## Certificate Type

Certificates issued by the Academy are designated as Certificates of Completion unless otherwise specified.

The certificate represents recognition of participation and demonstrated competence in the course content delivered by the Academy.

## Government Qualification or Professional Accreditation

The Master Enrollment Agreement states that Academy certificates do not constitute a government-issued qualification, licence, or professional accreditation unless explicitly stated and backed by the relevant regulatory authority.

## Withholding of Certification

The Academy may withhold certification where a student has not satisfied applicable academic or administrative obligations.

Financial obligations may also affect eligibility to receive certificates.

## Certificate Reissuance

Requests for duplicate or corrected certificates, including name corrections, may attract a reissuance fee.

Requests must be submitted in writing with valid identification.

The Academy is not responsible for delays caused by incorrect personal information provided during enrollment.

## No Automatic Entitlement

A student does not automatically receive a certificate simply because they enrolled in a course.

Certification is based on performance, participation, and fulfillment of applicable obligations.

Certification may be denied or revoked where it was obtained fraudulently or in breach of Academy policies.

## Important Information Rule

The chatbot should not promise that a student will receive a certificate before confirming that the applicable course requirements have been satisfied.

The chatbot should not describe a Torilo Academy Certificate of Completion as a government qualification, professional licence, or accreditation unless an approved source explicitly establishes that status.

## Attendance Expectations

Students are expected to attend and actively participate in scheduled Academy learning activities.

This includes:

- Scheduled classes
- Labs
- Assessments
- Related learning activities
- Group work
- Required assignments and coursework

Attendance and participation expectations apply to both in-person and virtual learning activities.

## Participation Requirements

Students are expected to:

1. Participate actively in scheduled learning activities.
2. Engage in required assignments and assessments.
3. Participate in required group work.
4. Submit coursework and projects by stated deadlines.
5. Maintain respectful and professional conduct in learning environments.

## Absence and Lateness

Repeated absence, lateness, or non-participation may result in academic consequences.

Possible consequences identified in the Master Enrollment Agreement include:

- Academic penalties
- Loss of access to resources
- Dismissal from the course

The specific consequence may depend on the circumstances and applicable Academy requirements.

## Course Completion

Successful course completion is dependent on meeting applicable requirements for:

- Attendance
- Participation
- Assignments
- Assessments

Students should not assume that enrollment alone is sufficient for course completion.

## Withdrawal

A student wishing to withdraw from a course must do so in writing within the withdrawal deadlines published by the Academy.

Late withdrawal or failure to formally notify the Academy may result in forfeiture of course fees and an "Incomplete" academic status.

## Academy-Directed Withdrawal

The Academy may withdraw a student from a course or programme for reasons including:

- Failure to meet participation expectations
- Failure to meet conduct expectations
- Non-payment of tuition or fees
- Academic dishonesty
- Disruption of the learning environment
- Health or behavioural concerns that compromise the integrity or safety of the course

## Important Information Rule

The chatbot should not invent a specific attendance percentage or absence limit unless that figure is present in an approved source.

If a student asks about a specific attendance threshold that is not contained in the knowledge base, the chatbot should state that the available policy does not specify the requested figure and direct the student to the appropriate Academy support channel for confirmation.

## General Refund Principle

Fees are generally non-refundable except where the Academy's Refund Policy provides otherwise.

## Refund Eligibility

Refunds are considered where:

1. The Enrolling Party submits a formal written withdrawal request.
2. The request is made within the applicable eligible timeframe.
3. The Student has not completed or accessed a substantial portion of the course or programme materials where applicable.
4. Applicable non-refundable fees are excluded from the refund.

## Standard Refund Schedule

### Withdrawal within 5 days after payment confirmation

A 100% refund may be provided, excluding applicable non-refundable fees.

The eligibility conditions described in this policy still apply.

## Special Circumstances

Refund requests arising from special circumstances, including medical emergencies, relocation, or other unforeseen situations, may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Supporting documentation may be required.

## Academy Cancellation

If the Academy cancels a course or programme, the agreement provides for:

- A full refund of paid fees; or
- The option to defer to another available session.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 14 working days of confirmation.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method or another method agreed by the parties.

## Non-Refundable Items

The agreement identifies certain non-refundable items, including:

- Learning materials already accessed or provided
- Third-party certification or examination fees that have already been remitted

## Misconduct

No refund is provided where a student has been expelled or dismissed from the Academy for reasons including:

- Academic dishonesty
- Misconduct
- Breach of the code of conduct
- Violation of Academy rules

## NEST Programme

The Master Enrollment Agreement contains specific refund provisions for programmes that include the NEST internship and career services option.

The portion of the programme fee relating to NEST internship and career services is stated to be non-refundable, including where the student does not successfully complete an internship, does not secure placement, or chooses to withdraw from participation.

## Refund Disputes

Disputes relating to refunds are addressed according to the dispute-resolution provisions of the Master Enrollment Agreement.

## Important Information Rule

The chatbot should not answer a refund question using only the phrase "5-day refund policy."

The answer should consider the conditions, exclusions, applicable non-refundable items, and other provisions contained in the approved refund policy.

For individual refund cases, the chatbot should not determine eligibility unless the required facts and applicable policy provisions are available.

Where the required information is unavailable, the student should be directed to the appropriate Academy support or admissions channel.

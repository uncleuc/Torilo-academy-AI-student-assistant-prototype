## General Conduct Expectations

Students are expected to maintain respectful and professional conduct in Academy learning environments.

Students are expected to comply with applicable Academy rules, policies, and conduct standards.

## Prohibited Behaviour

The Master Enrollment Agreement identifies the following behaviours as prohibited:

- Physical assault or fighting
- Threats or intimidation
- Verbal abuse toward staff, instructors, students, or guests
- Sexual harassment
- Stalking
- Inappropriate physical or verbal conduct
- Bullying
- Cyberbullying
- Harassment based on protected characteristics
- Vandalism
- Theft
- Damage to property
- Possession or use of weapons or illegal substances on Academy premises
- Conduct that violates applicable law

## Academic Integrity

Students are expected to comply with applicable academic integrity requirements.

Academic dishonesty may result in disciplinary action and may affect eligibility for certification or refunds.

## Disciplinary Action

Students who engage in prohibited behaviour may face disciplinary action.

Possible consequences identified in the Master Enrollment Agreement include:

- Suspension
- Expulsion
- Revocation of access to Academy facilities or platforms
- Withholding of certifications or academic records
- Forfeiture of fees paid without refund

The Academy may also report criminal or unlawful activity to appropriate law-enforcement authorities where applicable.

## Reporting Safety or Harassment Concerns

Students and Enrolling Parties are encouraged to promptly report concerns relating to:

- Safety
- Violence
- Harassment

The agreement states that reports will be handled with appropriate discretion and seriousness.

## Important Information Rule

The chatbot should provide policy information accurately and should not invent disciplinary procedures, penalties, reporting channels, or appeal processes that are not contained in the approved knowledge base.

For a specific disciplinary case, the chatbot should not make a determination about what action the Academy will take.

## Applying to Torilo Academy

Prospective students can begin the application process by completing the application form on the Torilo Academy website.

The public application page states that after an application is submitted, the admissions team will contact the applicant for a short conversation to discuss the application.

## Application Process

The publicly described application process is:

1. Complete and submit the online application form.
2. The admissions team contacts the applicant.
3. The applicant discusses their application with the admissions team.
4. The applicant receives information about the next steps.

The exact next steps may depend on the programme and the applicant's circumstances.

## Enrollment

The Master Enrollment Agreement states that enrollment into an Academy programme is governed by the terms of the Agreement and the applicable course registration process.

Enrollment may involve:

- Completion of the official enrollment or registration documentation.
- Providing accurate and verifiable personal, academic, and contact information.
- Meeting the applicable minimum age and programme eligibility requirements.
- Meeting any applicable prerequisites.
- Payment of applicable registration or course fees.
- Providing supporting documentation when requested.
- Compliance with applicable legal or immigration requirements where relevant.

## Admission Is Not Automatically Guaranteed

Submitting an application does not by itself guarantee admission.

The Master Enrollment Agreement states that admission may depend on factors including programme eligibility, capacity, class-size limitations, academic or behavioural history, and other institutional considerations.

The Academy reserves the right to deny, defer, or revoke admission where applicable requirements are not satisfied or where false or misleading information has been provided.

## Course Registration

Enrollment in the Academy does not automatically give a student access to every course.

Students must follow the registration procedures applicable to the specific course or programme.

Course registration may be subject to:

- Course availability
- Programme capacity
- Prerequisites
- Payment status
- Applicable registration procedures
- Other programme-specific eligibility requirements

## Application and Registration Information

Programme-specific information may differ between courses.

Students should confirm programme-specific requirements, schedules, fees, availability, and other current information with the admissions team before completing enrollment.

# Torilo Academy — Enrollment Requirements

- Document ID: enrollment_requirements

- Organization: Torilo Academy

- Last Verified: October 2026
- Status: Active

## Overview

Students seeking to enroll in a Torilo Academy programme must satisfy the applicable enrollment and programme requirements.

Requirements may vary depending on the course or programme.

## Enrollment Documentation

The Master Enrollment Agreement states that the Student or Enrolling Party must complete the official enrollment or registration form and provide accurate and verifiable information.

This may include:

- Personal information
- Academic information
- Contact information
- Emergency contact information
- Other information required by the Academy

## Minimum Age and Programme Eligibility

Students must meet the minimum age requirement and qualifications applicable to the specific course or programme.

Where prerequisites exist, supporting documentation may be required.

The exact age requirement or prerequisite for a specific programme should not be assumed unless it is stated in the approved programme information.

## Payment Requirements

Enrollment is not finalized until applicable registration fees, course fees, or initial deposits have been paid according to the Academy's payment terms or agreed payment plan.

Proof of payment may be required.

## Supporting Documentation

The Academy may request supporting documentation during enrollment.

Examples identified in the Master Enrollment Agreement include:

- Valid government-issued identification
- Academic certificates
- Passport photographs
- Signed copy of the Master Enrollment Agreement

Failure to provide required documentation may delay or prevent access to courses or Academy resources.

## International Applicants

International applicants and students subject to visa or immigration requirements must comply with applicable legal requirements relating to their study.

The Master Enrollment Agreement states that the Academy's responsibility is limited to the educational programme and does not extend to legal compliance beyond the programme.

## Admission Is Not Guaranteed

Meeting general enrollment requirements does not necessarily guarantee admission.

Admission may be affected by:

- Programme capacity
- Class-size limitations
- Programme eligibility
- Academic history
- Behavioural history
- Other institutional considerations

## Programme-Specific Requirements

Some courses may require:

- Prerequisite modules
- Baseline assessments
- Proof of technical competence
- Previous qualifications
- Other programme-specific requirements

The chatbot must use the specific course document when answering questions about prerequisites or eligibility for a particular course.

If the knowledge base does not contain a specific requirement, the chatbot should not invent one.

## Important Information Rule

General enrollment requirements should not be presented as requirements for every individual Torilo Academy course unless the relevant course information confirms them.

For programme-specific requirements, the chatbot should retrieve the relevant course document and use that information when answering.

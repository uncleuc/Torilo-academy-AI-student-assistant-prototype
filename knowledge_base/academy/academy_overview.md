## About Torilo Academy

Torilo Academy is a technology training academy offering intensive courses designed to help learners develop practical skills for technology and digital careers.

The academy describes its training as focused on web development, data, artificial intelligence, and other technology and digital disciplines.

Torilo Academy states that its courses are designed to help learners develop in-demand skills and pursue career opportunities in technology.

## Training Approach

Torilo Academy's public website describes a practical, career-oriented training approach.

Course pages commonly emphasize:

- Hands-on learning
- Practical projects
- Portfolio development
- Instructor guidance
- Industry-relevant tools and technologies
- Career preparation

The academy also provides different learning formats for selected programmes, including physical, live online, and hybrid learning options.

## Courses

Torilo Academy publicly lists courses across several technology and digital disciplines, including:

- AI Engineering
- Generative AI
- Backend Development
- Cloud Computing
- Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking
- Data Analysis
- Digital Marketing
- Frontend Web Development
- Product Design (UI/UX)
- Product Development
- Product Management
- Project Management
- Software Development
- Visual & Graphic Design

## Career and Portfolio Development

Torilo Academy's public course information frequently highlights practical projects and portfolio development.

The academy also states that it has a talent pool consisting of graduates who have received practical training across its courses.

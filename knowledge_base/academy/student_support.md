## Overview

Torilo Academy provides admissions and student-support channels for prospective and current students.

Students should use the appropriate contact channel depending on the nature of their enquiry.

## Admissions

The admissions team handles enquiries relating to applying to Torilo Academy and the admissions process.

**Phone:** +234 812 345 6971

**Email:** admission@torilo.ng

Students who submit an application through the public application page are contacted by the admissions team for a short conversation to discuss their application.

## Student Support

For student-support enquiries:

**Phone:** +234 701 570 8674

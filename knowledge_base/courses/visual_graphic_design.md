## Course Overview

Torilo Academy's Visual & Graphic Design programme develops advanced visual-design skills for creating graphics, brand identities, digital media and professional design assets.

## Duration

The Visual & Graphic Design programme is listed as an 8-week training programme.

## Learning Formats

The programme offers:

- Physical learning
- Live online learning
- Hybrid learning

## Curriculum

### Advanced Visual Design Principles

Topics include:

- Advanced colour theory
- Typography
- Experimental design techniques

### Adobe Creative Suite

Students learn advanced features in:

- Adobe Photoshop
- Adobe Illustrator
- Adobe InDesign

The curriculum also includes brochure design.

### Branding Design Principles

Topics include:

- Brand identities
- Logo design
- Typography systems
- Colour systems

### Brand Guidelines and Strategy

Topics include:

- Brand guidelines
- Style guides
- Brand strategy
- Positioning statements

### Brand Identity Systems

Topics include:

- Comprehensive brand identity systems
- Brand style guides
- Asset libraries

### Advanced Image Editing and Manipulation

Topics include:

- Advanced image-editing techniques
- Image manipulation
- Plugins
- Scripts

### Visual Design for Digital Media

Topics include:

- Immersive digital experiences
- Advanced digital-media design
- Motion-design fundamentals

### Final Project and Portfolio

Students apply the skills learned to a real-world project and develop a professional portfolio.

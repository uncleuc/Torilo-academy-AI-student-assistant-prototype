## Course Overview

Torilo Academy's Digital Marketing programme develops practical skills for using digital channels to reach audiences, acquire customers, improve engagement, and grow online visibility.

## Programme Options

The public course page lists:

- Digital Marketing Associate
- Digital Marketing Mastery + SEO
- Digital Marketing + NEST Package

The Associate programme is listed as 5 weeks, while the Mastery and NEST options are listed as 8 weeks.

## Learning Formats

The programme offers:

- Physical learning
- Live online learning
- Hybrid learning

## Curriculum

### Introduction to Digital Marketing

Topics include:

- Digital marketing fundamentals
- Audience segmentation
- Digital marketing channels
- Target audiences
- Customer personas
- Paid, earned and owned media
- Content writing

### Marketing Acquisition and Conversion

Topics include:

- Landing pages
- Lead generation
- Digital marketing UX
- Landing-page evaluation
- Business forms
- Conversion-rate optimisation

### Content Marketing and Social Media

Topics include:

- Local SEO
- Keyword research
- Search-volume analysis
- Competition analysis
- Google Ads
- Search-engine marketing
- Video marketing
- YouTube marketing
- Twitter/X marketing
- Facebook marketing
- Instagram marketing

### Paid Social and Advertising

Topics include:

- Campaign messaging
- Marketing metrics
- Social-media KPIs

- Social-media content
- Community management
- CRM

### Email Marketing and Customer Retention

### WordPress

Topics include:

- Business email

- Keyword research

The course covers:

- Google Analytics
- Google Search Console
- Google Tag Manager

### Certification Preparation

The course information includes preparation for Google certification examinations and, in the Mastery curriculum, Meta certification examination preparation.

### Artificial Intelligence

Students are introduced to AI concepts and tools that can:

- Automate tasks
- Generate insights
- Improve productivity
- Improve workflow efficiency

## NEST

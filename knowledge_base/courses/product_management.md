## Course Overview

Torilo Academy's Product Management programme develops practical knowledge for managing the development of successful products.

The programme covers product management fundamentals, product lifecycle, user needs, competitive analysis, customer development, experimentation, metrics, product development methodologies, and building products.

## Duration

The Product Management Mastery programme is listed as an 8-week programme.

The academy also lists a Product Management NEST option.

## Learning Formats

The programme offers:

- Physical learning
- Live online learning
- Hybrid learning

## Curriculum

### Introduction to Product Management

Topics include:

- Role of a Product Manager
- What is a product?
- Product managers and their responsibilities
- Different types of Product Manager roles
- Product versus Project Management

### Product Lifecycle and Development

Topics include:

- Product lifecycle
- Product development process
- Lean Product Development
- Agile
- Scrum
- Kanban
- Waterfall
- Real-world examples of development methodologies

### Ideas and User Needs

Topics include:

- Sources of product ideas
- Identifying real user needs
- Users versus customers
- Separating useful signals from noise

### Competitive and Market Analysis

Topics include:

- Finding competitors
- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Potential competitors
- Competitor analysis criteria
- Feature tables
- Competitor monitoring

### Customer Development

Topics include:

- Customer development
- Identifying people to interview
- Conducting customer interviews
- Interview questions
- Developing user personas
- Using customer information to inform product decisions

### Experimentation

Students are introduced to designing and running product experiments.

### Metrics

Students learn about defining success and measuring product results.

### Building the Product

Topics include:

- Project management for Product Managers
- Technology for Product Managers

### Artificial Intelligence

The programme introduces AI concepts and tools that can:

- Automate tasks
- Generate insights
- Improve productivity
- Improve workflow efficiency

## Portfolio Development

The course includes practical projects and portfolio development.

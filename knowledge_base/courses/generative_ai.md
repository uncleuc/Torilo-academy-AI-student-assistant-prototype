## Course Overview

Torilo Academy's Generative AI programme focuses on using generative artificial intelligence for business, productivity, creativity, automation, and practical AI applications.

The programme also covers prompt engineering and a range of AI tools.

## Duration

The Generative AI Mastery programme is listed as an 8-week intensive course.

The course page also displays a 16-week Generative AI Mastery option.

Students should confirm the currently available programme format with admissions.

## Learning Formats

The course offers flexible learning options, including:

- Physical learning
- Live online learning
- Hybrid learning

## Prompt Engineering

The programme teaches techniques for communicating effectively with AI systems.

Topics include:

- Prompt-engineering frameworks
- Persona, Context and Task prompting
- Few-shot prompting
- Techniques for improving AI-generated results
- Debugging poor or inaccurate AI responses

## Available Courses

Torilo Academy currently publicly lists training programmes in the following areas:

### AI Engineering

Focuses on building AI systems, understanding machine learning, working with AI engineering tools, and developing practical AI applications.

### Generative AI

Focuses on generative AI, prompt engineering, AI tools, automation, AI agents, and practical applications of generative AI.

### Data Analysis

Focuses on analysing and visualising data and using data to support business decision-making.

### Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking

Focuses on cybersecurity concepts, security operations, ethical hacking, penetration testing, threat analysis, and related security practices.

### Software Development

Focuses on coding skills required to design, build, and deploy software applications.

### Frontend Web Development

Focuses on building and optimising responsive, user-friendly web interfaces.

### Backend Development

Focuses on building server-side systems that support modern applications.

### Cloud Computing

Focuses on deploying and managing cloud infrastructure.

### Product Design (UI/UX)

Focuses on user-centred digital product design, UI principles, and UX methodologies.

### Product Management

Focuses on product development strategy from ideation through launch and beyond.

### Product Development

Focuses on developing digital products and applying product development practices.

### Digital Marketing

Focuses on SEO, content marketing, social media, and paid advertising.

### Visual & Graphic Design

Focuses on creating visual designs and graphics, including the use of AI tools where applicable.

### Project Management

Focuses on planning, leading, and delivering projects.

## Overview

Torilo Academy's student resources encourage practical, active, and organized approaches to learning.

## Practical Learning

Students are encouraged to apply concepts through projects and practical exercises rather than relying only on passive reading.

Project-based learning can help students connect concepts with practical application.

## Active Recall

Students can strengthen their understanding by attempting to recall information from memory.

Useful approaches include:

- Asking yourself questions about recently studied topics
- Testing your understanding without looking at your notes
- Completing practice exercises
- Revisiting areas where your recall is weak

## Peer Review

Peer review can help students receive feedback on their work and identify opportunities for improvement.

Students can share appropriate projects or assignments with peers and use constructive feedback to improve their work.

## Focused Study

Students can use structured study sessions to maintain focus.

The Pomodoro technique is one approach that uses focused work periods followed by planned breaks.

## Research and Note-Taking

Maintaining organized notes and research logs can help students keep track of:

- Important concepts
- Questions
- Research findings
- Useful resources
- Project information

## Learning Through Projects

Projects provide an opportunity to apply concepts in practical situations.

Students should use projects to reinforce concepts learned during their programme and build evidence of practical skills.

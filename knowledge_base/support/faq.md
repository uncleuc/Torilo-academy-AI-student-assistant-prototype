## What is Torilo Academy?

Torilo Academy is a technology and professional skills training institution offering programmes across areas including artificial intelligence, software development, data analysis, cybersecurity, cloud computing, product design, product management, digital marketing, graphic design, and project management.

## What courses does Torilo Academy offer?

Torilo Academy offers programmes in areas including:

- AI Engineering
- Generative AI
- Data Analysis
- Cybersecurity & Ethical Hacking
- Software Development
- Frontend Web Development
- Backend Development
- Cloud Computing
- Product Design (UI/UX)
- Product Development
- Product Management
- Digital Marketing
- Visual & Graphic Design
- Project Management

For the most current course catalogue, the chatbot should retrieve the course catalogue or relevant course document.

Students or prospective students can use the official Academy application process to apply for a programme.
